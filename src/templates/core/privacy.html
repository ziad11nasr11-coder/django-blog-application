Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Meridian collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you visit our website or use our blog features. By using the site, you agree to the practices described here.

Information We Collect

We may collect information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, account details, comments, contact messages, and newsletter preferences. We may also collect technical information such as browser type, device data, IP address, and usage activity.

How We Use Your Information

We use information to operate the site, manage accounts, publish and moderate comments, send requested updates, respond to messages, improve content quality, protect the service, and understand how readers interact with Meridian.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep the website secure, remember preferences, measure performance, and improve the reading experience. You can manage cookies through your browser settings.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. We may share limited information with service providers that help us operate the website, comply with legal obligations, prevent abuse, or protect the rights and safety of our readers and publication.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect information. No online service is completely secure, but we work to reduce risks and respond responsibly to issues.

Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as needed to provide services, maintain records, resolve disputes, meet legal obligations, and support legitimate editorial or operational purposes.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain uses of your personal information. You may contact us to make a privacy-related request.

Third-Party Services

Meridian may link to or use third-party services such as analytics, embeds, newsletter tools, or social platforms. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Children's Privacy

Meridian is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are made, we will update the last updated date and, where appropriate, provide additional notice.

Contact Information

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact the Meridian team.
