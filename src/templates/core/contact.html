FAQ

Before you write.

Can I pitch an article?

Yes. Send a concise summary, why it matters now, and links to recent work if you have them.

How do I report a correction?

Include the article URL, the detail that needs review, and any source material that helps verify the change.

Do you accept partnerships?

We consider editorially aligned collaborations, research briefings, events, and newsletter partnerships.

Will every message receive a reply?

We read every note. Replies depend on volume and whether the message needs direct follow-up.
