Cookies Policy

We use cookies to keep Meridian reliable, understand how readers use the site, and improve the reading experience without adding unnecessary noise.

Last Updated July 2026

This policy explains what cookies are, which categories we use, and how you can manage your preferences.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember basic information, improve performance, and provide a smoother experience across pages.

Types of Cookies We Use

Meridian uses a limited set of cookies to operate the website, measure performance, remember helpful preferences, and support trusted third-party services where needed.

E

Essential

Required for core site features such as security, sessions, and page delivery.

A

Analytics

Helps us understand reading patterns and improve content quality.

F

Functional

Remembers useful preferences that make the site easier to use.

M

Marketing

May support campaign measurement or relevant publication updates.

Essential Cookies

Essential cookies make the website work correctly. They may support authentication, security protections, form submissions, and basic site navigation. These cookies cannot usually be disabled without affecting how the website functions.

Performance & Analytics Cookies

Performance and analytics cookies help us understand which articles readers find useful, how pages perform, and where we can improve the site experience. We use this information in aggregate whenever possible.

Functional Cookies

Functional cookies remember choices such as display preferences, saved settings, or repeat interactions. They help make future visits feel more consistent and less repetitive.

Third-Party Cookies

Some embedded tools, analytics providers, or content services may set their own cookies. These third parties are responsible for their own cookie practices, and their policies may explain additional choices.

How We Use Cookies

We use cookies to keep the site secure, load pages efficiently, understand content performance, remember preferences, and improve editorial products such as newsletters and article recommendations.

Managing Cookie Preferences

You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings. Depending on your browser, you may be able to delete existing cookies, block new cookies, or receive alerts before cookies are stored.

Disabling Cookies

Disabling cookies may affect some parts of the website. Essential features may stop working, saved preferences may be lost, and some pages may not behave as expected.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookies Policy as our website, tools, or legal requirements change. When we make material updates, we will revise the last updated date shown on this page.

Contact Information

If you have questions about this policy or how Meridian uses cookies, please contact our editorial team.
